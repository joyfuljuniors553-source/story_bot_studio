# LegendraScope — true-story thumbnail style

Measured from examples/untold_life_napoleon.jpg (long) and
examples/short_untold_life_napoleon.jpg (Short). Movie-poster look: the person
large and close, a scene from their own story behind them, gold-and-silver
title, red brush stroke, navy ribbon.

## Colours
- #F9E075 Gold highlight (top of the name)
- #DD9C14 Gold mid
- #9A5A06 Gold shadow (bottom of the name)
- #F1F3F3 Silver light ("THE UNTOLD LIFE OF")
- #C6C8CB Silver mid
- #040E15 Outline dark (all title outlines and shadows)
- #B4100A Brush red
- #5E0B09 Brush red dark edge
- #081421 Ribbon navy
- #D4AB55 Ribbon border gold
- #F0E2B6 Ribbon text ivory
- #A00404 Tag red
- #720C0D Tag red dark
- #074359 Sky teal
- #012D3D Sky deep
- #F8B974 Sunset glow

## Long (1280x720)
- Person: head and shoulders, left 0-760 px, face about 300 px tall, eyes near
  y 300, looking up and to the right; uniform/clothes run off the bottom edge.
- Sky: stormy teal-navy clouds over the whole card; warm sunset glow only in
  the lower right.
- Scene from the person's story in the lower right (x 680-1280, y 430-720):
  e.g. battle, flags, cannon, a famous building. No faces, no text.
- Logo top left (x 20-275, y 10-100): gold globe arc above "LegendraScope",
  "Legendra" ivory, "Scope" gold.
- "TRUE STORY": red slanted tag flush in the top-right corner
  (x 995-1263, y 11-65), white heavy italic.
- Title column centred near x 900, top 10% to 62% of the card:
  "THE" / "UNTOLD" / "LIFE OF" in heavy serif capitals, chrome silver with a
  dark outline and drop shadow; the NAME on one line, the biggest text
  (cap height about 110 px), gold gradient, dark outline, bevel highlight.
- Red brush stroke under the lower half of the name, rising to the right.
- Ribbon under it (x 590-1232, y 375-450): navy, gold border, pointed ends,
  slightly rotated, three ivory serif words from the story ("RISE • POWER • DOWNFALL").

## Short (1080x1920)
- Logo top left, "TRUE STORY" top right, as on the long card.
- Person in the upper 55%, head near the top, centred.
- Title centred in the middle (about 40%-68% of the height), same styling, name
  on one line across nearly the full width.
- Ribbon right under the name (about 68%-75%).
- Story scene across the bottom quarter, sunset glow behind it.
